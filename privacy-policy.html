1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Civic Sparrow (“we”, “us”), of 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, handles personal information when you use civicsparrow.com, contact us, request a service or join our newsletter. Because many of our readers are students, we keep data collection to a minimum. You don't need an account to use any lesson or tool on this site. Questions are welcome at hello@civicsparrow.com or +1-888-777-5845.

2. What we collect

  • Messages and enquiries: your name, email address, optional organization name, the topic you choose and your message.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, device and browser type and approximate location, collected through cookies only if you choose to allow them.

Our quiz, Bill of Rights explorer and glossary run entirely in your browser. We don't record your answers or searches.

3. Students and children

Our lessons can be used by students of all ages without providing any personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our contact form asks that a parent, guardian or teacher send messages on behalf of younger children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to reply to messages, prepare and deliver services, send the newsletter you requested and keep basic records of bookings. With your consent, we use aggregated analytics to understand which lessons are most useful. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and we never sell or rent personal information.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and deliver services — such as our hosting provider, form and email services, newsletter platform and video-call platform for online sessions — and only as needed. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Google Analytics and advertising

If you choose “Accept all” in our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics and may display ads served by Google. We use Google Consent Mode so these tools do not set analytics or advertising cookies until you agree. Ads are never targeted to users we know to be children. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. Retention

We keep messages and enquiries for up to two years, booking records for up to seven years for accounting purposes, and newsletter details until you unsubscribe. Information is then deleted or anonymized.

8. Security

Our site uses encrypted HTTPS connections, and access to personal information is limited to the people who need it. No online system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will notify affected people where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, obtain a copy, object to certain uses and opt out of targeted advertising. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA. Email hello@civicsparrow.com to make a request; we'll respond within the time allowed by law.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The current version will always be on this page with its date at the top.
