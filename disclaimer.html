1. Educational information only

Civic Sparrow provides general educational information about government, civics and media literacy. Our lessons simplify complex topics so they're easier to learn, which means some details, exceptions and legal nuances are left out. Nothing on this website is legal advice. For questions about your specific rights or a legal situation, please consult a qualified attorney or the relevant government office.

2. Accuracy and changes

We work carefully to keep our content accurate and check it against primary and official sources. However, laws, court interpretations, procedures and officeholders change. Always confirm important details — such as election deadlines, registration rules or local procedures — with official sources like your state or local election office.

3. Nonpartisan content

Our aim is to explain, not to persuade. Descriptions of rights, cases and processes are not endorsements of any political party, candidate or policy. Where topics are debated, we try to describe the main viewpoints fairly and neutrally.

4. Summaries of legal texts

Plain-language summaries of the Constitution and its amendments are our own simplified explanations. They are not official interpretations. Read the full text at the National Archives for the exact wording.

5. Quiz and tools

Our quiz and interactive tools are learning aids. Scores are not an official assessment and are not recorded or shared.

6. External links

We link to trusted outside websites for further reading. We don't control their content or availability and aren't responsible for them.

7. Images

Photographs are licensed from Pexels and are used to illustrate topics. People and places shown in photos are not affiliated with Civic Sparrow and do not endorse our content.

8. Contact

If you spot an error or out-of-date information, please tell us at hello@civicsparrow.com. We review every report.
