Services

Civics learning, brought to your classroom and community

Our website is free for everyone. For schools, libraries and organizations that want more, we offer hands-on programs led by experienced educators.

Grades 6–12

Classroom workshops

Lively, discussion-based sessions that make civics feel relevant. Popular topics include “The Constitution in Everyday Life”, “How a Bill Becomes Law”, “Spotting Misinformation” and “Who Runs Your Town?”.

  • 45–90 minute sessions, in person in the New York area or online
  • Activities matched to your curriculum and grade level
  • Follow-up discussion questions for the next lesson
Enquire about a workshop
Teachers & school leaders

Teacher professional development

Practical training on teaching civics confidently: running respectful discussions on sensitive topics, using primary sources, and keeping lessons nonpartisan while still engaging.

  • Half-day or full-day sessions for teams
  • Ready-to-use discussion routines and classroom norms
  • Resource list and follow-up support
Enquire about training
Teachers & homeschool families

Lesson packs

Printable lesson plans that pair with each of our six learning units, with clear objectives, warm-up activities, primary-source excerpts and exit questions.

  • Written by our team, free of copied textbook material
  • Adaptable for 40- or 60-minute periods
  • Answer guides for teachers
Enquire about lesson packs
Youth groups & student councils

Youth civic projects

A guided program that helps young people choose a local issue, research it, hear different perspectives and present a respectful proposal to an appropriate audience.

  • Four to six sessions over a term
  • Research, interviewing and presentation skills
  • Emphasis on listening and fair debate
Enquire about youth projects
Libraries & community groups

Community talks

Friendly, nonpartisan talks for adult audiences, such as “Civics Refresher”, “Understanding Your Local Government” and “Reading the News Wisely”.

  • 60-minute talk with Q&A
  • Take-home one-page guide for attendees
  • Suitable for public libraries and community centers
Enquire about a talk
How booking works

Three simple steps

1. Tell us your goals

Share your audience, topic, group size and preferred dates using the form below.

2. Receive a proposal

Within two business days we send a written outline, format options and a clear price.

3. Confirm and prepare

Once you approve, we schedule the session and share any materials in advance. Nothing is booked until you confirm.
