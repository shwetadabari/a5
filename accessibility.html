Accessibility Statement

1. Our commitment

Civic education should be available to everyone. Civic Sparrow is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation or magnification. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

2. Accessibility features

  • A “Skip to content” link at the top of every page.
  • Clear heading structure so pages can be navigated by headings.
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Interactive tools — the branches explorer, Bill of Rights explorer and quiz — that work with a keyboard and announce updates to screen readers.
  • Readable text sizes, strong color contrast and visible focus outlines.
  • Layouts that adapt to phones, tablets and zoom levels without horizontal scrolling.
  • Reduced motion for visitors who prefer it in their device settings.

3. Known limitations

Some linked outside resources, such as embedded maps and external government websites, are not under our control and may not fully meet the same standards. We choose reputable sources and provide text alternatives where possible.

4. Feedback and help

If you have difficulty using any part of our website, or need a lesson in a different format, please contact us at hello@civicsparrow.com or +1-888-777-5845. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we'll aim to respond within two business days and provide the information in an accessible way.

5. Ongoing review

We review accessibility whenever we add new pages or tools and test with keyboard navigation and screen-reader software. This statement will be updated as we make improvements.
