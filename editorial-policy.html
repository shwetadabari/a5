1. Our editorial mission

Civic Sparrow publishes lessons that help people understand how government works and how to take part in public life. Every page should be accurate, balanced, easy to read and genuinely useful to a student, teacher or curious adult. This policy explains how we meet that standard.

2. Sources

We start with primary sources wherever possible: the U.S. Constitution, statutes, court decisions and official information from government bodies such as the National Archives, Congress and state election offices. We also consult respected educational references to check our explanations, and we link to sources so readers can go further.

3. Original writing and copyright

All lesson text, quiz questions and glossary definitions are written by our team. We do not copy passages from textbooks, websites or other publications. Short quotations from public-domain government documents are clearly identified. Photographs are used under the Pexels license, and we credit the source of any other material we are permitted to use.

4. Nonpartisanship and balance

  • We describe institutions and processes, not which side is right.
  • When a topic is debated, we present the main perspectives fairly and in neutral language.
  • We avoid loaded terms and explain any necessary technical terms.
  • We don't accept payment to promote any political view, organization or candidate.

5. Review and updates

Each lesson is checked against its sources before publication and reviewed at least once a year, or sooner when laws, procedures or court decisions change. Pages that describe processes with deadlines, such as elections, point readers to official sources for current dates.

6. Corrections

If you believe something is wrong or unbalanced, email hello@civicsparrow.com with the page and your concern. We review every request, correct genuine errors promptly and make meaningful corrections clear on the page.

7. Advertising

If our site displays advertising, ads are kept visually separate from lessons and have no influence on what we write.
